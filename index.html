About the park

A living space for biodiversity and learning

Biodiversity Park at Bhuteghar, Mahabaleshwar is the setting for this digital plant interpretation initiative. The project aims to help visitors learn about plants through individual QR-linked information pages.

Demonstration prototype: The five profiles currently shown are illustrative examples only. They are not confirmed members of the Forest Department's 60-plant collection. Official plant records, photographs and park information must be verified and approved before public launch.
