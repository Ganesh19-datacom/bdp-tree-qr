About the park

A living space for biodiversity and learning

Biodiversity Park at Bhuteghar, Mahabaleshwar is the setting for this proposed digital plant interpretation initiative. The project aims to help visitors learn about the plants they encounter through individual QR-linked information pages.

Demonstration prototype: The five plant profiles shown here are illustrative examples only. They are not confirmed members of the Forest Department's 60-plant collection. Official park history, department details, logos and the verified plant list will be added after approval.
