BDP-004

Arjun Tree

Terminalia arjuna (Roxb. ex DC.) Wight & Arn.
Demonstration content: This is a sample species profile for website presentation. It is not confirmation that this species is part of the BDP collection. Botanical details must be checked and approved by the Forest Department before public use.

About this plant

Arjun is a large tree often associated with riverbanks and moist habitats. It is recognised by its spreading crown and pale, smooth to flaky bark.

Leaves

Leaves are simple, usually opposite or nearly opposite, oblong and leathery, often with two small glands near the leaf base.

Flowers

Small greenish-white flowers are borne in spikes or panicles.

Fruits

The fruit is a woody, fibrous, winged structure, usually with five prominent wings.

Ecological importance

The tree can help stabilise soil in suitable riparian locations and provides shelter and resources for insects and other organisms.

Did you know?

Its bark has a distinctive layered appearance and is often used as a field identification feature.
