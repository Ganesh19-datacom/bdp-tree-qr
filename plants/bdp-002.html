BDP-002

Banyan

Ficus benghalensis L.
Demonstration content: This is a sample species profile for website presentation. It is not confirmation that this species is part of the BDP collection. Botanical details must be checked and approved by the Forest Department before public use.

About this plant

The Banyan is a large fig tree famous for its spreading crown and aerial roots that grow down from branches and may develop into supporting trunks. It is culturally important across India.

Leaves

Leaves are simple, alternate, broad and leathery, with a prominent midrib.

Flowers

The tiny flowers are enclosed inside a fleshy structure called a syconium (fig), so they are not usually seen as typical open flowers.

Fruits

Small figs develop in pairs or clusters and may turn reddish when ripe.

Ecological importance

Banyan figs are an important food source for birds, bats and other fruit-eating animals. Its large canopy offers shade and habitat.

Did you know?

A single banyan can spread over a very large area as aerial roots become additional supporting trunks.
