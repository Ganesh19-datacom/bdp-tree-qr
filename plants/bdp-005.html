BDP-005

Teak

Tectona grandis L.f.
Demonstration content: This is a sample species profile for website presentation. It is not confirmation that this species is part of the BDP collection. Botanical details must be checked and approved by the Forest Department before public use.

About this plant

Teak is a large deciduous tree known for its broad leaves and durable timber. It is cultivated in many tropical regions and is an important forest and plantation species.

Leaves

Leaves are very large, simple and opposite, with a rough upper surface and often a softly hairy underside.

Flowers

Numerous small white flowers are borne in large terminal clusters.

Fruits

The fruit is a small, rounded drupe enclosed by an inflated, papery calyx.

Ecological importance

Teak forms part of tropical seasonal forest ecosystems and provides canopy cover and habitat when growing in mixed forests.

Did you know?

Teak leaves can be exceptionally large, especially on young shoots and coppice growth.
