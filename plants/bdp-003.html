BDP-003

Peepal / Sacred Fig

Ficus religiosa L.
Demonstration content: This is a sample species profile for website presentation. It is not confirmation that this species is part of the BDP collection. Botanical details must be checked and approved by the Forest Department before public use.

About this plant

Peepal is a long-lived fig tree recognised by its heart-shaped leaves with a distinctive long tapering tip. It is widely planted and has strong cultural significance in South Asia.

Leaves

Leaves are alternate, broadly heart-shaped and have a long, slender drip tip.

Flowers

As in other figs, tiny flowers are enclosed inside the fig structure (syconium).

Fruits

Small rounded figs grow in pairs or clusters and darken as they mature.

Ecological importance

Its figs are eaten by birds and other wildlife. The tree provides shade and supports urban and rural biodiversity.

Did you know?

The long leaf tip helps rainwater drain away from the leaf surface.
