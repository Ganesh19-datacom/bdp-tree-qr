BDP-001

Jamun / Indian Blackberry

Syzygium cumini (L.) Skeels
Demonstration content: This is a sample species profile for website presentation. It is not confirmation that this species is part of the BDP collection. Botanical details must be checked and approved by the Forest Department before public use.

About this plant

Jamun is a medium to large evergreen tree known for its dense crown and edible purple-black fruits. It is commonly found in many parts of India and is valued for shade, food and ecological functions.

Leaves

Leaves are simple, opposite, glossy and leathery, with a characteristic aromatic smell when crushed.

Flowers

Small, pale greenish-white to pinkish flowers occur in clusters. Flowering generally takes place in the warmer months, varying by location.

Fruits

Fruits are oval to oblong berries that turn deep purple or almost black when ripe.

Ecological importance

Flowers can provide resources for insects, while fruits are eaten by birds, bats and other wildlife. The tree also provides shade and contributes to urban and rural green cover.

Did you know?

The ripe fruit is widely eaten fresh and is also used in regional foods and beverages.
